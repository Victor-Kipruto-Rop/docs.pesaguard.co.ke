Integration guides

M-Pesa (Daraja) Live

The current integration scope validates and matches M-Pesa (Safaricom Daraja) STK Push and PayBill callbacks for reconciliation.

Use the current contract

Callback request details and transaction fields must come from the API reference and the OpenAPI document served by your deployment. This guide does not define a separate payment-initiation contract or invent provider payloads.

  • Confirm the callback and transaction contract against your deployment before integration.
  • Use the reconciliation concepts to understand matching and exceptions.
  • For outbound notifications from PesaGuard, follow the separate webhook delivery and signature verification guides.
Keep inbound and outbound flows distinct

M-Pesa provider callbacks are inbound to PesaGuard. PesaGuard webhooks are outbound to your receiver and have their own signature format. The Developer Platform's sandbox transaction listing is read-only; it does not process or simulate payments.
