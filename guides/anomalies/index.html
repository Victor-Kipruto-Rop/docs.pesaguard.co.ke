Anomaly guides

Anomaly guides Live

Anomaly signals describe patterns for review. They add context to a transaction; they are not automatic fraud verdicts or a substitute for a human decision.

  • Start with the signal: review the named pattern, severity, and supporting context.
  • Keep the decision with people: a signal prompts review; a reviewer records the outcome and reason.
  • Check the contract: event-specific payload fields are still being stabilized, so verify them against the current deployment contract.
