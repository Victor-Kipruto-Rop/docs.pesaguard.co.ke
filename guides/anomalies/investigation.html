Anomaly guides

Investigation Live

Investigate a flagged transaction using the named signal and its supporting context, then record a human decision and reason in the audit trail.

  1. Review the signal name, severity, evidence window, and observed values.
  2. Compare the signal with the transaction and tenant context; do not treat the flag as a fraud verdict.
  3. Record the reviewer, decision, and reason so the outcome remains auditable.

Blocking, if any, is a recorded human decision. See the anomaly concept and audit-trail guidance for the review boundary and evidence context.
