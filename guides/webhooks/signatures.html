Webhook guides

Signatures Live

PesaGuard outbound webhooks use a timestamped HMAC-SHA-256 signature over canonical JSON. Verify the sender and timestamp before persisting or acting on the event.

  • Use the endpoint's signing secret and the documented canonicalization rules.
  • Compare the expected digest in constant time and enforce a freshness window.
  • Deduplicate the accepted event so retries do not repeat business effects.

This signature format applies to PesaGuard outbound webhooks. Inbound payment-provider callbacks may use different formats; do not reuse this verifier for them. Follow the detailed verification reference and retry guidance.
